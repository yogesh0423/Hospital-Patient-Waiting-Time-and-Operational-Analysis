# 🏥 Hospital Patient Waiting Time & Operational Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes patient feedback and operational factors related to the hospital waiting experience.

The objective is to understand patterns in patient satisfaction, waiting delays, registration processes, staff availability, crowd issues, appointment systems, communication, and digital queue support.

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** is developed to transform the analyzed data into meaningful KPIs, visualizations, and business insights that can support hospital operational decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Long waiting times and inefficient patient-flow processes can negatively affect patient satisfaction and the overall hospital experience.

The project aims to analyze patient responses and identify operational areas that may require further investigation or improvement.

### Key Business Questions

- What is the overall level of patient satisfaction?
- How do patients perceive waiting delays?
- How common are registration delays?
- How do patients perceive staff availability?
- What crowd-related issues are reported?
- How effective is the appointment system?
- How is digital queue support perceived?
- Do patient experiences differ across age groups?
- Are waiting delays associated with overall satisfaction?
- Which operational areas should management investigate further?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Clean and prepare the hospital patient dataset.
2. Understand patient demographics and operational responses.
3. Analyze waiting-related experiences and satisfaction.
4. Identify patterns across different patient groups.
5. Develop meaningful KPIs for hospital operations.
6. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
7. Generate data-driven business insights.
8. Provide recommendations based on the observed patterns.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **82 patient responses** and includes demographic, waiting-experience, operational, and satisfaction-related variables.

### Main Data Categories

#### Patient Demographics
- Gender
- Age
- Age Group

#### Waiting Experience
- Waiting Delay
- Waiting Comfort
- Overall Satisfaction

#### Operational Factors
- Staff Availability
- Registration Delay
- System Efficiency
- Crowd Issues
- Communication

#### Technology & Appointment Factors
- Appointment System
- Digital Queue Support
- Peak Staff

> **Note:** The dataset is survey-based and does not contain timestamp-level patient flow records. Therefore, the analysis focuses on reported waiting experience and operational perceptions rather than calculating actual waiting duration in minutes.
